The Beginning

From Libya, With Everything.

You left behind everything familiar — your family, your home, your language, the streets you grew up on — and stepped into a country that didn't know your name, didn't speak your tongue, and didn't owe you a single thing.

Most people never understand what that truly takes. To build a life from a blank page, in a place you didn't choose, with people you didn't know, in a language you were still learning — and to do it not for yourself, but for children who weren't even old enough to understand what sacrifice meant.

You built something beautiful, Mama. And you built it from nothing but love and faith.

"She didn't just raise a family. She built a home — out of faith, out of grit, and out of a love so steady it never needed saying."
Your Legacy

Look What You Built.

Whether your health had its hiccups, or you quietly felt the weight of being far from your own family — the meals were always cooked. The clothes were always there. The house was always known in our community as the most generous, most welcoming place anyone had ever walked into. You never took a day off from being our mom.

Always

I know we have our differences. I always knew that. But what I didn't know — what I only started to truly understand after moving out and being on my own — is just how much you were doing every single day without ever making it a thing.

This is the first Mother's Day we spend apart. I always thought of you as superwoman — and I still do — but now I get it. Now I understand the real weight behind it all. And I want you to know: Anis and I are missing you. Every single day.

I notice your absence in everything. In the quiet of a morning with no food already made. In a hard day with no one to call who truly, fully knows me. In every little thing I never stopped to notice when you were right there.

"Through thick and thin, Mama — we all love you. May Allah bless every struggle, every tear shed, and every sacrifice you have ever made for us."

Until Then

Inshallah, Mama — this is just a chapter. Not the whole book.

Anis will graduate and come back. I will move back after this company takes off — after I can stand in front of you and say I built something I'm proud of. And we will all be reunited. In the same city. Around the same table.

Until then, stay strong for us. You've always been so unbelievably strong — and now it really gets tested. But I am so confident you will make it through, because everything you have ever done has been for the sake of us. And that is a strength no distance can ever touch.

Happy Mother's Day, Mama.
I love you.
